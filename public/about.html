THE PROJECT

Why Portivo exists

Network teams often move between separate tools, terminal sessions, spreadsheets and incomplete evidence when diagnosing or changing infrastructure.

Portivo brings inventory, live port visibility, endpoint discovery, interactive SSH, controlled operations, automation, auditing and power awareness into one consistent interface. Its purpose is not to hide the network. It is to give operators a clearer way to understand it, act carefully and preserve what happened.

The project is designed for self-hosted operation so organizations can keep control of access, credentials and operational data within their own environment.

PROJECT PRINCIPLES

How the platform is shaped

01

Operator clarity

Present live state, risk and evidence in a form that supports confident operational decisions.

02

Controlled change

Preview, scope and audit actions before configuration is changed across the fleet.

03

Independent operation

Remain self-hosted, transparent and usable without handing infrastructure control to an external service.

04

Evidence over assumptions

Prefer verified device data and explicit uncertainty instead of inventing unsupported conclusions.
