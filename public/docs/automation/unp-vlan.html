Automation

UNP and VLAN maintenance

Governed AOS-aware maintenance workflows.

v2.0.0Source-backed

Check UNP

Check UNP is a reporting/read workflow that can inspect relevant UNP state across selected targets.

Fix UNP

Fix UNP is a controlled change action and is available only where the current AOS family and exact action policy permit it. AOS 8-only UNP actions are not exposed as executable AOS 6 commands.

VLAN Port Maintenance

The VLAN maintenance workflow combines selected port maintenance behavior with safeguards and scope. It is a governed change action available to Operator-level policy and higher where permitted.

Safeguards

Automation safeguards are persisted independently and exposed through dedicated endpoints for Fix UNP and VLAN maintenance.
